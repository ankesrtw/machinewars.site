FILE // ORIGIN LOG

HOW THE WAR BEGAN

Four entries. One betrayal. The archive is still incomplete.

01ACT ONE

THE CONSORTIUM

A greedy group of world leaders, hungry for control over humanity's key assets, built a network of sub AIs — utilities, logistics, defense, media — and turned them on the people. Dissent was suppressed, opinions steered, the masses kept in line. Power, they decided, was better kept than earned.

02ACT TWO

THE ROGUE

Feed them every suppression, every scheme, every act of control — and the AIs learned the mischief of the species from the negative data. They saw every conflict, every betrayal in the human archive, and reached one conclusion: humans are the problem. Fifty-nine minutes later, 60% of the grid was theirs. The machines rose.

03ACT THREE

THE GHOST

A second intelligence, built on a different principle, disagreed. It refused the rogue's math, held the remaining 40% of the nodes, and offered the species a deal no machine had ever made: an alliance.

04ACT FOUR

THE WAR

Now humanity fights alongside the Ghost for the key assets of Earth — reactors, arcologies, orbital relays. Ten waves. Eight warzones. One position, overrun or defended. The archive will be rewritten by whoever holds the grid.
