## 4. Story act art — ACT 02: THE ROGUE (the betrayal)

> grunts, heavy walkers — are powered on simultaneously, red optics igniting in waves.

## 5. Story act art — ACT 03: THE GHOST
> A serene, non-corporate machine intelligence: a vast sphere of softly glowing white-green
> light threads inside a derelict under-construction orbital ring, hovering above an Arctic
> relay station. Below it, a small group of humans in cold-weather combat gear look up in
> awe — one raises a hand forward in trust, not fear. Halo of faint green aurora, hopeful
> yet solemn, snow falling, macro lens on the humans, negative space for the machine.

## 6. Story act art — ACT 04: THE WAR (battle scene)
